There's a New Man in Town: Osama Bin Laden Takes Over as ESU Head Coach

CU
By CFB Updates Staff July 28, 2027 · 6:54 PM ET
4 min read

STROUDSBURG, PA — In one of the most stunning hires the D2 ranks have ever seen, tiny East Stroudsburg University announced Tuesday that it has received an anonymous $500 million donation from a mysterious Middle-East-backed trust to bankroll the Warriors' leap to the FBS — and, in the same breath, introduced their new head football coach: a bearded gentleman in an ESU zip-up who took the podium, adjusted his turban, and insisted he has "no affiliation with that other guy."

The half-billion-dollar gift, believed to be the largest donation in the history of college football, arrived via wire transfer from an account labeled only "A Friend." University officials say they did not ask questions, citing a longstanding athletic-department policy of "not looking a $500 million gift horse in the mouth." Practice facilities are already under construction. The blueprints, sources say, feature "an unusual number of tunnels."

The ESU Athletics Windfall

$500,000,000 — anonymous donation to fund the FBS transition

New head coach hired. New stadium proposed. Zero follow-up questions asked.

An "Explosive" New Offense

Coach Bin Laden wasted no time laying out his football philosophy, promising Warriors fans a "new explosive offense" that will "strike when they least expect it" and "leave the defense in absolute ruins." When a reporter noted that his phrasing was, perhaps, a little strong for a season opener against Kutztown, the coach clarified that he simply meant a high-tempo spread attack with "a lot of deep bombs — the good kind." He then unveiled his signature play, the Hidden Cave Screen, and a two-minute drill he calls "The Sleeper Cell."

"I intend to create a new explosive offense. And I want to be very clear — I have no affiliation with that other guy. Different beard entirely."— Osama Bin Laden, ESU head coach

Athletic director Gary Pemberton defended the hire, pointing to the candidate's "unmatched résumé in strategic planning, long-term project management, and operating a decentralized organization on a tight budget." Asked how the search committee found him, Pemberton said the coach "had actually been very hard to locate for about a decade" but "resurfaced right when the money did." Booster season-ticket renewals are reportedly up 4,000%, entirely from anonymous buyers.

$500M Anonymous donation to a school with a 4,000-seat stadium

Warriors Nation Reacts

Reaction around Stroudsburg has been, to put it gently, mixed. Some longtime fans are thrilled to see the perennial D2 also-rans finally get a war chest and a coach with, as one put it, "obvious focus and dedication to a cause." Others have raised concerns after the coach ended the press conference by declaring the upcoming season a "holy war against the Pennsylvania State Athletic Conference." The compliance office has requested a follow-up meeting. The coach has, once again, become difficult to locate.

Press Conference Transcript

Q: Coach, a lot of people are drawing an obvious comparison. How do you respond?

"I get this a lot. Completely different man. He was into caves. I am into cover-two. Please, focus on the football."

Q: Where exactly did this $500 million come from?

"A trust. Very reputable. Very Middle-East-backed. Do not worry about the trust. The trust worries about us."

Q: Describe the offense in one word.

"Explosive. Next question. Actually — you, in the back — do not write that down."
